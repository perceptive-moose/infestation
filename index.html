FIELD GUIDE

The keep, by touch.

Clear each room of rats. Everything moves when you do, so there’s no rush.

Directional pad
Move in four directions. Your sword turns with you. Hold to keep moving.
Wait
Pass one turn without moving. In a two-hero room, both heroes wait.
OK
Start, enter a selected room, choose a menu item, or continue after a win.
Undo / Restart
Undo as often as you need. Tap Restart twice within two seconds to confirm.
Two heroes
Select hero 1 or 2. For simultaneous moves, turn Sync on, choose hero 1’s direction, switch to hero 2, and choose their direction. Turn Sync off for ordinary moves. Turning Sync off does not erase a move already queued; choose a new direction to replace that hero’s input. Wait replaces both queued inputs with a waiting turn.
Map & menus
Tap the game’s buttons and map rooms directly; drag the map to explore. The pad and OK work in menus too.
Room tips
Tips appear above the controls without moving or resizing the puzzle. Scroll a long tip to read it all. Tap − to minimize it, then Room tip to reopen it.
More room
The game removes its duplicate portrait instruction panel while the touch controls are visible. Use ▤ to hide the touch controls and restore the game's own buttons. If a menu is cut off, rotate to portrait and hide the controls; the pad and OK can also navigate menus.
A playable prototype

This runs the original game and its levels. The game’s own hints still mention keyboard keys. Progress is saved in this browser, separately from the original site. For two heroes using these touch controls, disconnect physical controllers.
